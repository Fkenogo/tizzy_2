## Database Structure

### Tier System
- **Tier 1:** Body Region (Upper Body, Lower Body, Core, Full Body)
- **Tier 2:** Primary Focus (Strength, Cardio, Mobility & Flexibility, Balance & Stability, Power & Explosiveness)
- **Tier 3:** Specific Exercise Name

### Tags
Each exercise includes:
- **Muscles Targeted:** Specific muscle groups worked
- **Difficulty Level:** Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced
- **Training Goals:** Primary fitness objectives
- **Equipment:** Required tools or bodyweight only
- **Metric:** How to measure (Reps, Time, Distance)
